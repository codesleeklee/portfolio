Featured projects

CapGains

Built on Bubble.io

Tax-incentive intelligence for venture investors and startups

A complex fintech platform that helps VCs, angels, shareholders and the companies they back qualify for and maximize QSBS (Section 1202) and Section 1244 tax benefits, using guided questionnaires, AI document analysis and automated diagnostics.

  • Fintech
  • AI
  • Crunchbase API
  • Backend Workflows
  • PDF reports
Read the case study

The problem

Founders and investors routinely lose valuable tax breaks on startup equity because the qualifying rules are technical, depend on years of company history, and are easy to trip over without noticing. Gathering the evidence (financing rounds, balance sheets, stock agreements, operating locations) is slow, manual work for lawyers and analysts.

What I built

A multi-portal platform where investors, companies and CapGains analysts collaborate on the same data:

  • Investor portal to track a portfolio of securities, see each holding's eligibility status and holding period, and get alerts when something puts a benefit at risk.
  • Company portal with a step-by-step onboarding wizard that captures legal entities, parent and acquired entities, funding history, patents, office and employee locations, and financial statements.
  • Guided questionnaires for QSBS (company and shareholder) and Section 1244, with answers that feed directly into the diagnostics.
  • Issuer, security and shareholder diagnostics that score each criterion, flag risky answers and track every data point back to its source document.
  • Generated reports, including a QSBS report and a 1244 loss report, built from dozens of reusable report sections and exportable to PDF.
  • Admin and analyst tooling for coverage requests, security requests, reconciliation of schedules of investments, bulk emails and team invitations.

Bubble skills shown

Complex relational database design, privacy rules on sensitive financial data, recursive and scheduled backend workflows, API Connector at scale, AI integration, reusable-element architecture, multi-role portals, PDF generation.

GreenPortfolio

Built on Bubble.io

See how your investments impact the planet

An investment app that connects to users' brokerage and bank accounts through Plaid, scores their holdings on climate impact, and matches them with climate-focused financial advisors.

  • Fintech
  • Plaid
  • Stripe
  • Backend Workflows
  • Analytics
Read the case study

The problem

Investors increasingly care where their money goes, but brokerages don't tell you how your funds and banks rank on climate. Answering that question meant exporting statements and researching every holding by hand.

What I built

  • Plaid account linking for investment and depository accounts, importing accounts, holdings, securities and balances, with refresh and re-sync flows.
  • CSV upload path for users whose institution isn't on Plaid, with a multi-step import that creates securities, holdings and accounts and merges duplicates.
  • Climate scoring: per-holding and per-bank environmental metrics, a portfolio climate score, peer-group and benchmark comparisons, leaf ratings and a Climate Hub with contextual tips.
  • Advisor matching: an advisor portal and investor matching flow that calls an external matching API and shows matched advisors with their philosophy, services, fees and booking link.
  • Subscriptions with Stripe tiers, upgrade prompts and cancellation handling.
  • Weekly digest emails and an admin back office for banks, sectors, funds, metrics, peer groups, tips and subscriptions.

Bubble skills shown

Plaid integration, multi-step data pipelines in backend workflows, Stripe subscriptions, external API matching, JWT and Google Cloud auth, CSV import and export, data visualization, analytics instrumentation.

Cloud-IQ

Built on Bubble.io

One control centre for IoT SIMs, devices and customers

A web and native mobile platform that lets an IoT connectivity reseller manage thousands of SIM cards across multiple network providers, track devices on a live map, bill customers and run support, all from one dashboard.

  • IoT
  • Telecom APIs
  • Native mobile
  • Maps
  • Stripe
  • Backend Workflows
Read the case study

The problem

An IoT connectivity business buys SIMs from several wholesale networks, each with its own portal, and resells them to customers who also need GPS trackers, invoices and support. Staff were jumping between supplier portals and spreadsheets to answer basic questions like "where is this device" or "how much data has this SIM used".

What the platform does

  • Unified SIM inventory that pulls SIMs from two different wholesale providers into one list, with usage, status, network, location and provisioning history per SIM.
  • Full SIM lifecycle control from inside the app: activate, cancel, swap by ICCID or MSISDN, bar and unbar, roaming settings, IMEI lock, tariff holidays, SIM groups and data caps.
  • SMS centre to send single or bulk SMS to devices and read SMS history.
  • Live location: SIM and cell-tower location plus GPS tracker positions on Google and Leaflet maps, with geocoding and routes.
  • Commercial layer: products, supplier and customer tariffs, SIM orders, RFQs, invoices with partial payments, Stripe billing and a customer self-service portal.
  • Support desk with tickets and comments, synced to the client's Monday.com support board.
  • Native mobile app (14 mobile views) with a dashboard, inventory, SIM detail, SMS centre, bulk SMS and support chat.

Bubble skills shown

Large-scale API integration, token refresh, scheduled syncs, multi-tenant privacy rules, maps and geolocation, Bubble native mobile, billing.

Critmetic

Built on Bubble.io

A learning platform for critical digital literacy

An education platform with separate portals for students, facilitators and school staff, combining a course library, assignments and assessments, a moderated social feed, chat and a wellness space.

  • EdTech
  • Multi-role portals
  • Social
  • Notifications
  • Backend Workflows
Read the case study

The problem

Teaching young people to think critically about technology needs more than a video course. Facilitators need to run classes, set and grade work and keep students engaged, and schools need oversight, all in a safe space.

What the platform does

  • Four portals: student, facilitator, staff and class, each with its own navigation and permissions.
  • Curriculum builder: modules, lessons, videos, readings with versions and reading levels, visuals, slide decks and a resource library with collections, folders and keyword search.
  • Assignments and assessments: multiple choice, matching and open questions, polls, submissions with attachments, feedback, due-date reminders and CSV exports of results.
  • Social learning: a feed with posts, reposts, likes, hashtags, @mentions, link previews and media; announcements; group and direct chat; flagging and reporting for moderation.
  • Wellness and growth: meditations and guided practice with audio, playlists, "future self" goals and milestones, notes with tags.
  • Notification engine for new assignments, feedback, mentions, messages and reminders by email and in-app.

Bubble skills shown

Complex role-based portals, reusable-element architecture, social features, scheduled notifications, CSV import pipelines, privacy rules for minors' data.
